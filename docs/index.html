An epistemic web, in motion

Six people and one agent share one mesh of signed rows. Each sees a view of the same rows, folded through their own standing, and each view generates a queue: exactly the actions the admission door would accept from them now. Pick a person, take an action, and watch every other queue shift. Nothing here is a list of buttons: the queue is the door run forwards.

The rule, from the CIRIS Constitution 4.5.13: for an object, the node holds its family, its lifecycle state and the person's standing toward it; the family's transitions (verb, from, to, roles, policy, widens) come from the family model; the queue is the enabled transitions, one item each, and the door admits by the same predicate. Proved in formal/event_lifecycle; the transitions below are rows of manifests/transitions.json; the story is tools/mesh_story.py. Widening items are confirmed before signing; narrowing ones take one step; a stranger to an object sees no item on it.
