Population Opt-In ChoicesBenefits (+) & Downsides (−)

1. Link shaping — server (7,000 B/s)Default: ON 95%
+ Hides row timing/size in channel MDU frames (ρ=0.5, W=600 s); carries onion walks at +0 B extra wire cost. − Sends ~18.1 GB/mo/link continuous cover even idle; adds ~1.16 s mean (~2.3 s @ 16 KB TCP MDU) Poisson wait.
2. Link shaping — home (2,800 B/s)Default: OFF 25%
+ Masks home traffic timing/size (W=600 s) on direct 1-hop links; expands standing shaped cover pool for onion peers. − Costs ~7.2 GB/mo/link continuous cover bandwidth; adds ~2.9 s mean (~5.8 s @ 16 KB TCP MDU) wait.
3. Link shaping — phone-wifi (1,400 B/s) / phone-cellular (350 B/s)Default: OFF 10%
+ Shapes direct 1-hop mobile links (W=600 s Wi-Fi, W=3,600 s cellular); contributes mobile shaped cover hops. − 3.6 GB/mo/link Wi-Fi (0.9 GB/mo/link cell, ~7 GB/mo @ 8 links); radio awake; 11.6–46.5 s TCP MDU delay.
4. Offer relay (directly reachable server/home/phone-charging)Default: ON 90%
+ Each directly reachable home/server adds L−1 = 7 shaped slots (Tiers.lean: each_home_adds_seven, reach ≠ capacity) & avoids stranding at the 73rd peer. − Carries strangers' sealed rows up to class cap; discloses signed route & locality to ≤3 referred strangers.
5. Announce owner-binding on rosterDefault: ON 85%
+ Reachable by short ciris:// name; can join public rooms (open:self_admit) & serve as announced relay/seat. − Lists device on public federation roster (lightnet); declining (self darknet) requires manual v3 contact codes.
6. Anonymous mode / Onion tier (CC 5.4.6)Default: OFF 12%
+ Unlinkable federation publishing & private rooms via 3-hop X-Wing onion walks over shaped links from sticky guard. − Row plane only (≤64 KiB, no live A/V); ~1/3 home shaped share cover cost; never relays; uptime-correlation on sleeping phones.
7. Onion relay participation (3-layer deal)Default: ON 88%
+ Expands 3-layer relay deal & mixing pool so anonymous walks aren't narrowed by a small volunteer set. − Layer links must run shaped, reserving up to 4 of the node's L=8 shaped link slots at class rate.
8. Publishable H3ERE agent traces (CC 4.5.13)Default: OFF 15%
+ Contributes 14 KiB/decision scrubbed reasoning traces to cohort accountability and RATCHET evaluation. − Widens trace visibility beyond self/family and adds signing, scrubbing, and chunk custody storage load.

How the Rulebook & Transport Tiers Globe Works
Every person and device sits on populated land (2,961 density cells stitched to continent polygons) and runs the same constitutional rulebook (CC 4.5.13). Per CC 5.4.3 ("A shaped link is one hop"), shaped budget classes (server 7,000 B/s, home 2,800 B/s, phone-wifi 1,400 B/s, phone-cellular 350 B/s, seat 33,000 B/s at ρ=0.5) are 1 direct hop and never forwarded; peers without a direct path carry on ordinary links. Large rooms fan out via the CC 6.1.6 deterministic-alm member relay tree over sealed chunks (distinct from CC 5.3.3.2's no-SFU-relay-role; no seat decodes or selects). 15,296 B X-Wing onion packets replace cover frames on shaped links at 0 extra wire bytes (CC 5.4.6).
